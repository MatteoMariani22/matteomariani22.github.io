About me

Building products
that make an impact.

I am a Senior Mobile & Frontend Engineer with over 10 years of experience in designing and scaling Mobile and Web applications. I am currently serving as a Team Lead at Globant, where I guide cross-functional teams through critical technological migrations to Flutter for major insurance groups like Allianz.

I act as the primary technical point of contact for clients, driving decisive, high-level strategies for both the architectural design of new applications and the continuous evolution (Change Requests) of live apps on the stores.

  • 🏛Architecture & Quality: Expert in complex architectures (BLoC), Clean Code, and end-to-end Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) management.
  • 💼Industries: Solid background working with institutional and banking clients (Banca d'Italia, Generali).
  • 🎓Education: Currently completing a degree in Computer Engineering to consolidate my Software Architecture foundations.
